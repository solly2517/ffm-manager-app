# FFM Manager and Delegate Operations

## Cover
FFM User Guide
Manager and Delegate Operations
A practical visual guide to secure field-force work

## Slide 1
### FFM has two operational workspaces
- Manager: plan, assign, monitor, communicate, and review operational work.
- Delegate: complete assigned visits and record reports, GPS, and evidence.
- Every route requires secure authentication.
- Administrator procedures are outside this guide.

## Slide 2
### Start securely, then work from your role
- Open the FFM site and select **Sign in securely**.
- Confirm the correct account and role after login.
- Manager workspace: `/`
- Delegate workspace: `/delegate`
- Sign out on shared devices.

## Slide 3
### Managers control the operational picture
- Dashboard cards show live delegates, task activity, pending work, and coverage.
- Delegates, Clients, Tasks, Messages, Reports, Geography, and Stock Review are available from the sidebar.
- Manager access is limited to the administrator-assigned delegate team.
- Empty states mean no current records are available; they are not automatically errors.

## Slide 4
### Managers turn plans into assigned work
- Create clients with accurate account, city, and contact details.
- Add doctors linked to the correct client.
- Create tasks with a client, assigned delegate, and scheduled date/time.
- Update task status only when the operational state is true.

## Slide 5
### Reports make decisions traceable
- Use From and To dates to focus the operational summary.
- Review task, visit, completion, and coverage metrics.
- Export the selected range as CSV when required.
- Protect exported files because they may contain sensitive operational information.

## Slide 6
### Delegates follow a visit from task to closure
- Open **My Tasks** and confirm the client, time, and assignment.
- Start the visit and allow location access when required.
- Complete the report while details are fresh.
- Check out only after the report and required evidence are saved.

## Slide 7
### GPS and evidence create a reliable record
- Check-in and check-out save timestamps and GPS coordinates.
- Capture only authorized photo, audio, and signature evidence.
- Obtain consent and follow retention rules.
- Wait for visible success feedback; a local draft is not the same as a server save.

## Slide 8
### Delegates stay aligned through Messages and Plan
- Messages carries operational updates and follow-up instructions.
- Plan shows submitted visit plans and review statuses.
- Surgeries stores verified clinical-operation details.
- Do not mark a record complete before the real work or documentation exists.

## Slide 9
### Stock Review is view-only
- Stock Review links to the Al Tamam Stock Management application.
- FFM exposes no stock adjustment, transfer, or deletion controls.
- If the view shows login, use the approved external sign-in path.
- Do not rely on screenshots or manually entered numbers as live stock values.

## Slide 10
### Use a safe daily routine
- Manager: review dashboard, assignments, pending tasks, messages, reports, and follow-ups.
- Delegate: check tasks, verify client, GPS check-in, report, evidence, GPS check-out, and messages.
- Escalate missing assignments, failed saves, GPS problems, or authentication issues.
- Never share passwords, invitation links, client information, or evidence with unauthorized people.

## Slide 11
### When something goes wrong
- Sign-in problem: verify account, invitation, connection, and browser session.
- Missing task: confirm the correct account, date, and manager assignment.
- GPS problem: enable location services and retry from a clear-signal area.
- Upload/report problem: check connection, wait for status feedback, and retry safely.
- Contact the FFM Administrator for account, role, assignment, or unresolved stock-app issues.

## Closing
Work accurately. Save evidence deliberately. Escalate early.
