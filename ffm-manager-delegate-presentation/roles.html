01 / OPERATING MODEL
FFM has two operational workspaces
One secure platform, two role-specific ways of working.
MANAGER

Coordinate the network

Plan work, assign tasks within the administrator-defined team, monitor progress, communicate, review visits, and export operational reports.

DELEGATE

Complete the field visit

Open assigned tasks, check in with GPS, record the visit, upload authorized evidence, communicate updates, and check out.

ALL ROUTES REQUIRE AUTHENTICATION · ADMINISTRATOR PROCEDURES ARE OUTSIDE THIS GUIDE
