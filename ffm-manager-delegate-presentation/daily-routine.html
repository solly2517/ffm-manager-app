09 / DAILY OPERATING RHYTHM
Use a safe daily routine
A consistent start and close keeps field work visible, accurate, and recoverable.
MANAGER

Coordinate the day

STARTReview Dashboard, assignments, pending tasks, and important messages.
DURINGMonitor task status, respond to field updates, and review follow-up needs.
CLOSECheck incomplete work, review reports, and export only approved date ranges.
DELEGATE

Complete the visit

STARTCheck My Tasks, confirm the client and time, and review the route.
DURINGGPS check in, write the report, capture authorized evidence, and save successfully.
CLOSEGPS check out, confirm the visit state, then review Messages and Plan.
Escalate early: report missing assignments, failed saves, GPS problems, authentication issues, or unresolved stock-app access. Never share passwords, client information, or evidence with unauthorized people.
ACCURACY FIRST · SAVE DELIBERATELY · ESCALATE EARLY
