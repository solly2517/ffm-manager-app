# Saudi holiday planning sources

- Saudi Central Bank, Holidays and Working Hours: https://www.sama.gov.sa/en-US/Supervision/pages/holidayschedules.aspx
  - Publishes official annual financial-institution holiday schedules, including Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha, National Day, and Founding Day.
- Saudipedia, Official Holidays in Saudi Arabia: https://saudipedia.com/en/official-holidays-in-saudi-arabia
  - Confirms fixed annual National Day on 23 September and Founding Day on 22 February.
  - Identifies Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha as official holidays whose dates follow the Hijri calendar and annual official schedules.

Implementation rule: show fixed-date holidays deterministically. Treat Eid-date indications as official schedule data only when an annual government or Saudi Central Bank schedule has supplied the Gregorian date range; label dates as subject to official confirmation where appropriate.

For 2026, the Saudi Central Bank financial-institution schedule lists Eid al-Fitr from 17 March through 23 March and Eid al-Adha from 24 May through 28 May. These dates are used as annual planning indicators for 2026 only; future religious-holiday ranges must be updated from an official annual schedule.
